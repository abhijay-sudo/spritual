# Original Spritual concept illustrations

Created with OpenAI image generation for this app landing page on 19 October 2026.

- **Ayodhya at dawn**: an imagined river city, terracotta and ochre architecture, layered emerald vegetation, pale ivory sky, muted lapis water. Original: `originals/ayodhya-dawn.png` (1672 × 941).
- **The forest conversation**: an imagined meeting of Valmiki and Narada beneath a banyan tree beside a lotus pond, in a miniature-inspired illustrated-book treatment. Original: `originals/forest-sages.png` (1024 × 1536).
- **The forest journey**: Rama and Lakshmana accompany Vishvamitra into a layered indigo and teal forest, beside a winding river under a saffron sky. Created for version two. Original: `originals/forest-journey.png` (1672 × 941). Used in the hero cover, third world scene, and social metadata.

These are original concept illustrations, not historical reproductions, manuscript plates, or documentation of a specific real location. Text, handset frames, controls, and book typography are live HTML/CSS rather than text generated inside the images.

Production WebP copies are in `dist/assets/`. The mechanical format conversion retained the original compositions; the forest conversation export is 900 px wide for the displayed sizes. The two landscape exports retain their original dimensions. CSS crops adapt the art to the page and handset. The original PNG files are preserved here for future production work.

The existing Spritual sunrise SVG and bundled fonts were recovered separately from the project source. See `dist/assets/ASSET_PROVENANCE.json` and the adjacent font license files.
