# Spritual app introduction

## Current user direction — 19 October 2026

Build one sophisticated website page first. Its purpose is to introduce the Spritual app, give visitors a tangible glimpse of the reading features, and direct them to the app when distribution is available.

The single primary commercial action is **Get Spritual**. With no verified store URLs and the app still pre-release, this opens a clear, useful launch-status dialog. No signup or payment is simulated. When real store URLs are supplied, one release configuration enables the corresponding download links.

Warm archival paper (#FAF7F2), forest ink, original painted landscapes, generous negative space, confident editorial serif typography. Each section adds a new part of the story. No fabricated testimonials, ratings, audience counts, scarcity, prices or availability.
