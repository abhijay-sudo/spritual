Spritual is an illustrated story app in the making. Our first journey draws on the Valmiki Ramayana, with connected chapters in English and Hindi.

The stories on this page pair original illustrations with short English and Hindi retellings from the Valmiki Ramayana and Bhagavata Purana. Each story carries its source. The app’s opening follows Valmiki’s question to Narada in Bala Kanda, Sarga 1.
