Festivals and their stories

Each festival on the calendar, paired with a story from the library to read that day.

  1. Tuesday, 24 November 2026Dev Deepawali

    Lamps on the ghats of the Ganga at Varanasi on the night of Kartik Purnima. The river in Shiva's hair

  2. Friday, 15 January 2027Makar Sankranti

    Pilgrims bathe at Gangasagar, where tradition says Ganga, following Bhagiratha, reached the sea. The river in Shiva's hair

  3. Saturday, 6 March 2027Maha Shivaratri

    A night kept for Shiva, who gave Ganga a way to the earth. The river in Shiva's hair

  4. Monday, 22 March 2027Holi

    Holika Dahan the night before, then a day of colours, kept above all in Vraja, Krishna’s home. The mountain lifted

  5. Thursday, 15 April 2027Ram Navami

    Rama’s birthday, and the question that opens his story. The bow that broke the sky

  6. Tuesday, 20 April 2027Hanuman Jayanti

    Hanuman’s birthday on the Chaitra full moon, as kept in North India; other regions keep it on other days. The leap begins here

  7. Sunday, 18 July 2027Guru Purnima

    A full moon for teachers, remembered through Vyasa, who gave us the Mahabharata. The dog at heaven's door

  8. Wednesday, 25 August 2027Janmashtami

    Krishna’s birthday, and the rain he sheltered a village from; some calendars keep it the night before. The mountain lifted

  9. Saturday, 4 September 2027Ganesh Chaturthi

    Ganesha’s day, and the journey he found a shorter way around. Seven times around

  10. Thursday, 30 September 2027Navratri

    Nine nights for the Goddess, and two requests made beside a river. Clay, river, goddess

  11. Saturday, 9 October 2027Vijayadashami

    Rama’s victory, and the long road home that follows it. Through fire to Ayodhya

  12. Friday, 29 October 2027Diwali

    Lamps lit, tradition says, to welcome Rama, Sita and Lakshmana home to Ayodhya. Through fire to Ayodhya

  13. Saturday, 13 November 2027Dev Deepawali

    Lamps on the ghats of the Ganga at Varanasi on the night of Kartik Purnima. The river in Shiva's hair

Dates are for New Delhi, taken from Drik Panchang. Some festivals are kept a day apart, or on other days, in other regions.
