RamayanaBala Kanda

The bow that broke the sky

A sage asks if one perfect man exists. Narada answers with a name. Far away, a king lifts a child from a furrow, a prince learns to call back the weapons of the gods, and in Mithila a bow no god could string breaks like thunder.

RamayanaAyodhya Kanda

Fourteen years, two sandals

On the eve of his crowning, Rama is sent to the forest. Sita and Lakshmana follow, Guha keeps watch by the Ganga, and Bharata climbs to Chitrakuta to give back a throne he never wanted.

  • Friendship

RamayanaAranya Kanda

The deer, the vulture, the elder

A golden deer lures Rama away, Ravana carries Sita into the sky, and an old vulture fights to his last breath. Then the grieving brothers walk west, to a hermitage where an old woman has waited years to welcome them.

  • 6 min
  • Hospitality
  • Devotion

RamayanaKishkindha Kanda

The leap begins here

A disguised Hanuman meets two armed strangers by Pampa, a friendship is sworn around fire, an arrow brings down Vali, and at the ocean's edge the son of the Wind remembers his strength.

  • Communication

RamayanaSundara Kanda

Across the sea, a ring

Hanuman leaps a hundred yojanas of ocean, outwits the jaws of Surasa, forces the gate of Lanka and finds Sita under the Ashoka trees, defying Ravana with a blade of grass. Then, from a branch above her, he begins to speak of Rama.

  • Courage
  • Persistence

One vanara wrecks Ravana's grove, lets Indrajit bind him, sets Lanka ablaze with his own tail, and carries Sita's jewel home to Rama.
