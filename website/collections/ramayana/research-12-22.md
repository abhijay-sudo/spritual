# Ramayana illustrated collection: chapters 12–22

Research and original bilingual drafting: 26 October 2026. **Local editorial draft.** No named human reviewer, exact-version approval, legal clearance or permission for publication is asserted by this file.

## Scope and source method

- Owns only `chapters-12-22.mjs` and this research note. Earlier approved story files have not been edited or used as approval for these new versions.
- Eleven connected chapters, each in four scenes. The final chapter closes the abridgement at Yuddha Kanda's return and consecration. It is not a complete verse translation, does not include every episode and does not include Uttara Kanda.
- Original English narrative and Hindi counterpart, not scraped or copied modern translation prose. No invented direct dialogue. Literary transitions are original editorial language, not quotations from Valmiki.
- Main check: the ancient Sanskrit chapter sequence presented with explanatory translations at ValmikiRamayan.net. The modern English translations and commentary on that site have copyright notices; public availability is not reuse permission. They were consulted to check narrative beats. Do not import their prose, glossary, comments, images or page assets into the release.
- Historical comparison: R. T. H. Griffith, *The Ramayan of Valmiki*, 1870–1874, Gutenberg ebook 24869. The bibliographic record, Book V Canto I and the concluding Book VI return/consecration passage were inspected. Gutenberg calls its edition public domain **in the USA**; that label does not prove worldwide commercial clearance. Full HTML exceeded the web reader's size limit; the same public URL was retrieved through ordinary HTTPS with `curl` for targeted passage inspection, without bypassing access controls or disabling TLS.
- Distinguish the ancient narrative from a modern critical edition, historical verification or archaeological proof. Geography, divine appearances, transformations, aerial travel and restorations are presented as the epic's narrative, without scientific or historical claims.
- URLs and chapter labels are exported in each chapter's `sources`. The source site occasionally has introductory copy or verse-number typos; consult the verse sequence, not an introduction alone. Numbering differs between editions; Griffith's concluding Canto CXXX is not silently equated with the Sanskrit site's sarga 130.

## Visual staging guidance for these chapters

- 12: fire-witnessed alliance → identifying garland → dialogue after the shot → coronation. Do not make Vali's fatal injury a looping action spectacle.
- 13: ring → dim cavern opening → Sampati at the coast → Hanuman on Mahendra. Jambavan is an active guide, not scenery.
- 14: leap → welcoming Mainaka → small Hanuman at Surasa's open mouth → distant Lanka. Preserve `ocean-mainaka` and `ocean-surasa` scene IDs.
- 15: night skyline → careful search → Sita's refusal → concealed messenger. Sita is clothed and composed according to the scene, not presented for voyeuristic viewing.
- 16: cautious questions → ring → deliberation → jewel entrusted. Preserve `ring-choice` and `ring-jewel`; one Hanuman, one Sita. Do not show Sita flying away, because she declines that offer.
- 17: court → city fire kept distant and non-graphic → returning shore → jewel/report. The fire scene should allow a still alternative.
- 18: Vibhishana's arrival → discussion → ocean → organised construction. Construction imagery follows the named Nala account.
- 19: embassy → medicinal mountain → decisive battle at a restrained distance → mourning and new responsibility. No invented gore or numerical troop claims in art captions.
- 20: Sita stopping retaliation → public address → still symbolic fire boundary → reunion. Do not animate entry into flames or turn suffering into an interactive achievement. A quiet portrait/assembly image is safer and truer to the reading purpose.
- 21: restored company → Pushpaka over remembered places → hermitage → messenger at Nandigrama. No claim of an exact modern map.
- 22: welcome → sandals → consecration → a calm closing page. Celebration need not be perpetual particles or flashing lights.
