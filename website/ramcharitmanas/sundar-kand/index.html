Shloka

शान्तं शाश्वतमप्रमेयमनघं निर्वाणशान्तिप्रदंśāntaṃ śāśvatamaprameyamanaghaṃ nirvāṇaśāntipradaṃ

ब्रह्माशम्भुफणीन्द्रसेव्यमनिशं वेदान्तवेद्यं विभुम्।brahmāśambhuphaṇīndrasevyamaniśaṃ vedāntavedyaṃ vibhum /

रामाख्यं जगदीश्वरं सुरगुरुं मायामनुष्यं हरिंrāmākhyaṃ jagadīśvaraṃ suraguruṃ māyāmanuṣyaṃ hariṃ

वन्देऽहं करुणाकरं रघुवरं भूपालचूड़ामणिम्।।1।।vande'haṃ karuṇākaraṃ raghuvaraṃ bhūpālacūṛāmaṇim //

Meaning I bow to Rama, the lord of the world, the best of the Raghus and the crown jewel of kings. He is peaceful, eternal, beyond all measure and free of sin; he gives the peace of liberation. Brahma, Shambhu and the lord of serpents serve him without pause. He is known through Vedanta and pervades everything. He is the teacher of the gods, Hari who through his maya appears as a man, and a mine of compassion.

Shloka

नान्या स्पृहा रघुपते हृदयेऽस्मदीयेnānyā spr̥hā raghupate hr̥daye'smadīye

सत्यं वदामि च भवानखिलान्तरात्मा।satyaṃ vadāmi ca bhavānakhilāntarātmā /

भक्तिं प्रयच्छ रघुपुङ्गव निर्भरां मेbhaktiṃ prayaccha raghupuṅgava nirbharāṃ me

कामादिदोषरहितं कुरु मानसं च।।2।।kāmādidoṣarahitaṃ kuru mānasaṃ ca //

Meaning O Raghupati, there is no other longing in my heart. I speak the truth, and you, the inner self of every being, know it. O finest of the Raghus, grant me full and overflowing devotion, and make my mind free of desire and the other faults.

Shloka

अतुलितबलधामं हेमशैलाभदेहंatulitabaladhāmaṃ hemaśailābhadehaṃ

दनुजवनकृशानुं ज्ञानिनामग्रगण्यम्।danujavanakr̥śānuṃ jñānināmagragaṇyam /

सकलगुणनिधानं वानराणामधीशंsakalaguṇanidhānaṃ vānarāṇāmadhīśaṃ

रघुपतिप्रियभक्तं वातजातं नमामि।।3।।raghupatipriyabhaktaṃ vātajātaṃ namāmi //

Meaning I bow to the son of the Wind. He is the home of matchless strength, and his body shines like a mountain of gold. He is a fire to the forest of demons and the foremost of the wise. He holds every virtue, leads the monkeys, and is the beloved devotee of Raghupati.

Moved by Jambavan's words, Hanuman climbs a mountain by the sea and leaps for Lanka. Mainaka rises from the water to offer him rest; Hanuman touches it, bows, and says there is no rest for him until Rama's work is done. The gods send Surasa, mother of serpents, to test him. He grows, she grows; then he becomes tiny, passes through her mouth and out again, and she blesses him.

जामवंत के बचन सुहाए। सुनि हनुमंत हृदय अति भाए।।jāmavaṃta ke bacana suhāe / suni hanumaṃta hr̥daya ati bhāe //

तब लगि मोहि परिखेहु तुम्ह भाई। सहि दुख कंद मूल फल खाई।।taba lagi mohi parikhehu tumha bhāī / sahi dukha kaṃda mūla phala khāī //

जब लगि आवौं सीतहि देखी। होइहि काजु मोहि हरष बिसेषी।।jaba lagi āvauṃ sītahi dekhī / hoihi kāju mohi haraṣa biseṣī //

सिंधु तीर एक भूधर सुंदर। कौतुक कूदि चढ़ेउ ता ऊपर।।siṃdhu tīra eka bhūdhara suṃdara / kautuka kūdi caṛheu tā ūpara //

जेहिं गिरि चरन देइ हनुमंता। चलेउ सो गा पाताल तुरंता।।jehiṃ giri carana dei hanumaṃtā / caleu so gā pātāla turaṃtā //

जिमि अमोघ रघुपति कर बाना। एही भाँति चलेउ हनुमाना।।jimi amogha raghupati kara bānā / ehī bhā̃ti caleu hanumānā //

जलनिधि रघुपति दूत बिचारी। तैं मैनाक होहि श्रमहारी।।jalanidhi raghupati dūta bicārī / taiṃ maināka hohi śramahārī //

Doha

हनूमान तेहि परसा कर पुनि कीन्ह प्रनाम।hanūmāna tehi parasā kara puni kīnha pranāma /

राम काजु कीन्हें बिनु मोहि कहाँ बिश्राम।।1।।rāma kāju kīnheṃ binu mohi kahā̃ biśrāma //

Meaning Jambavan's words were pleasing, and Hanuman's heart delighted in them. Brothers, he said, wait for me, enduring hardship on roots and fruit, until I return having seen Sita; the task will succeed, and I feel great joy. Bowing to all, holding Raghunatha in his heart, he set out gladly. He leapt in play onto a fine mountain by the shore and, remembering Raghubir again and again, the mighty son of the Wind sprang up; the mountain under his feet sank at once into the underworld. Hanuman sped like Raghupati's unfailing arrow. The ocean, knowing him Raghupati's messenger, told Mainak to ease his weariness. Hanuman touched the mountain, bowed, and said: until Rama's work is done, how can I rest?

जात पवनसुत देवन्ह देखा। जानैं कहुँ बल बुद्धि बिसेषा।।jāta pavanasuta devanha dekhā / jānaiṃ kahũ bala buddhi biseṣā //

सुरसा नाम अहिन्ह कै माता। पठइन्हि आइ कही तेहिं बाता।।surasā nāma ahinha kai mātā / paṭhainhi āi kahī tehiṃ bātā //

आजु सुरन्ह मोहि दीन्ह अहारा। सुनत बचन कह पवनकुमारा।।āju suranha mohi dīnha ahārā / sunata bacana kaha pavanakumārā //

राम काजु करि फिरि मैं आवौं। सीता कइ सुधि प्रभुहि सुनावौं।।rāma kāju kari phiri maiṃ āvauṃ / sītā kai sudhi prabhuhi sunāvauṃ //

तब तव बदन पैठिहउँ आई। सत्य कहउँ मोहि जान दे माई।।taba tava badana paiṭhihaũ āī / satya kahaũ mohi jāna de māī //

कबनेहुँ जतन देइ नहिं जाना। ग्रससि न मोहि कहेउ हनुमाना।।kabanehũ jatana dei nahiṃ jānā / grasasi na mohi kaheu hanumānā //

जोजन भरि तेहिं बदनु पसारा। कपि तनु कीन्ह दुगुन बिस्तारा।।jojana bhari tehiṃ badanu pasārā / kapi tanu kīnha duguna bistārā //

सोरह जोजन मुख तेहिं ठयऊ। तुरत पवनसुत बत्तिस भयऊ।।soraha jojana mukha tehiṃ ṭhayaū / turata pavanasuta battisa bhayaū //

जस जस सुरसा बदनु बढ़ावा। तासु दून कपि रूप देखावा।।jasa jasa surasā badanu baṛhāvā / tāsu dūna kapi rūpa dekhāvā //

सत जोजन तेहिं आनन कीन्हा। अति लघु रूप पवनसुत लीन्हा।।sata jojana tehiṃ ānana kīnhā / ati laghu rūpa pavanasuta līnhā //

बदन पइठि पुनि बाहेर आवा। मागा बिदा ताहि सिरु नावा।।badana paiṭhi puni bāhera āvā / māgā bidā tāhi siru nāvā //

मोहि सुरन्ह जेहि लागि पठावा। बुधि बल मरमु तोर मै पावा।।mohi suranha jehi lāgi paṭhāvā / budhi bala maramu tora mai pāvā //

Doha

राम काजु सबु करिहहु तुम्ह बल बुद्धि निधान।rāma kāju sabu karihahu tumha bala buddhi nidhāna /

आसिष देह गई सो हरषि चलेउ हनुमान।।2।।āsiṣa deha gaī so haraṣi caleu hanumāna //

Meaning The gods saw the Wind-son going and, to test his strength and wisdom, sent Surasa, mother of serpents. The gods gave me today's meal, she said. Hanuman said: let me do Rama's work and bring the Lord Sita's news; then I will enter your mouth. Truly, mother, let me go. She refused; he said: then swallow me. She opened her mouth a yojana; he doubled. At sixteen he became thirty-two; however wide she grew, he grew twice that. At a hundred he turned tiny, went in, came out, bowed and asked leave. She said: as the gods wished, I have tested your wisdom and strength. You will do all Rama's work. Blessing him, she left; Hanuman went on gladly.

A demoness in the sea who seizes birds by their shadows catches Hanuman's; he slays her and reaches the far shore. From a hill he sees Lanka, a city of gold walls and countless guards. Taking a form as small as a mosquito and remembering Rama, he goes in at night. Lankini, the city's guardian, stops him; one blow brings her down, and she recalls Brahma's word that this would mark the end of the demons, and blesses his errand.

निसिचरि एक सिंधु महुँ रहई। करि माया नभु के खग गहई।।nisicari eka siṃdhu mahũ rahaī / kari māyā nabhu ke khaga gahaī //

जीव जंतु जे गगन उड़ाहीं। जल बिलोकि तिन्ह कै परिछाहीं।।jīva jaṃtu je gagana uṛāhīṃ / jala biloki tinha kai parichāhīṃ //

गहइ छाहँ सक सो न उड़ाई। एहि बिधि सदा गगनचर खाई।।gahai chāhã saka so na uṛāī / ehi bidhi sadā gaganacara khāī //

सोइ छल हनूमान कहँ कीन्हा। तासु कपटु कपि तुरतहिं चीन्हा।।soi chala hanūmāna kahã kīnhā / tāsu kapaṭu kapi turatahiṃ cīnhā //

ताहि मारि मारुतसुत बीरा। बारिधि पार गयउ मतिधीरा।।tāhi māri mārutasuta bīrā / bāridhi pāra gayau matidhīrā //

तहाँ जाइ देखी बन सोभा। गुंजत चंचरीक मधु लोभा।।tahā̃ jāi dekhī bana sobhā / guṃjata caṃcarīka madhu lobhā //

नाना तरु फल फूल सुहाए। खग मृग बृंद देखि मन भाए।।nānā taru phala phūla suhāe / khaga mr̥ga br̥ṃda dekhi mana bhāe //

सैल बिसाल देखि एक आगें। ता पर धाइ चढेउ भय त्यागें।।saila bisāla dekhi eka āgeṃ / tā para dhāi caḍheu bhaya tyāgeṃ //

उमा न कछु कपि कै अधिकाई। प्रभु प्रताप जो कालहि खाई।।umā na kachu kapi kai adhikāī / prabhu pratāpa jo kālahi khāī //

गिरि पर चढि लंका तेहिं देखी। कहि न जाइ अति दुर्ग बिसेषी।।giri para caḍhi laṃkā tehiṃ dekhī / kahi na jāi ati durga biseṣī //

अति उतंग जलनिधि चहु पासा। कनक कोट कर परम प्रकासा।।ati utaṃga jalanidhi cahu pāsā / kanaka koṭa kara parama prakāsā //

चउहट्ट हट्ट सुबट्ट बीथीं चारु पुर बहु बिधि बना।।cauhaṭṭa haṭṭa subaṭṭa bīthīṃ cāru pura bahu bidhi banā //

गज बाजि खच्चर निकर पदचर रथ बरूथिन्ह को गनै।।gaja bāji khaccara nikara padacara ratha barūthinha ko ganai //

बहुरूप निसिचर जूथ अतिबल सेन बरनत नहिं बनै।।bahurūpa nisicara jūtha atibala sena baranata nahiṃ banai //

बन बाग उपबन बाटिका सर कूप बापीं सोहहीं।bana bāga upabana bāṭikā sara kūpa bāpīṃ sohahīṃ /

नर नाग सुर गंधर्ब कन्या रूप मुनि मन मोहहीं।।nara nāga sura gaṃdharba kanyā rūpa muni mana mohahīṃ //

कहुँ माल देह बिसाल सैल समान अतिबल गर्जहीं।kahũ māla deha bisāla saila samāna atibala garjahīṃ /

नाना अखारेन्ह भिरहिं बहु बिधि एक एकन्ह तर्जहीं।।nānā akhārenha bhirahiṃ bahu bidhi eka ekanha tarjahīṃ //

कहुँ महिष मानषु धेनु खर अज खल निसाचर भच्छहीं।।kahũ mahiṣa mānaṣu dhenu khara aja khala nisācara bhacchahīṃ //

एहि लागि तुलसीदास इन्ह की कथा कछु एक है कही।ehi lāgi tulasīdāsa inha kī kathā kachu eka hai kahī /

रघुबीर सर तीरथ सरीरन्हि त्यागि गति पैहहिं सही।।raghubīra sara tīratha sarīranhi tyāgi gati paihahiṃ sahī //

Doha

पुर रखवारे देखि बहु कपि मन कीन्ह बिचार।pura rakhavāre dekhi bahu kapi mana kīnha bicāra /

अति लघु रूप धरौं निसि नगर करौं पइसार।।3।।ati laghu rūpa dharauṃ nisi nagara karauṃ paisāra //

Meaning A sea-demoness ate flying creatures, seizing their shadows on the water. She tried this on Hanuman; he saw through it, killed her, crossed the ocean. He saw lovely woods, humming bees, fruiting trees, birds and beasts, and fearlessly climbed a great mountain. Uma, says Shiva, it is not the monkey's might but the Lord's glory, which devours Death. From the peak he saw Lanka: a towering sea-girt fortress, jewelled golden ramparts, markets, streets, countless elephants, horses, chariots, mighty demon troops of many forms, gardens, pools, maidens enchanting sages, mountain-like wrestlers, guards, demons devouring cattle and men. Tulsidas tells of them because, slain by Raghubir's arrows, they will reach the true goal. Seeing the guards, he resolved to enter tiny, by night.

मसक समान रूप कपि धरी। लंकहि चलेउ सुमिरि नरहरी।।masaka samāna rūpa kapi dharī / laṃkahi caleu sumiri naraharī //

नाम लंकिनी एक निसिचरी। सो कह चलेसि मोहि निंदरी।।nāma laṃkinī eka nisicarī / so kaha calesi mohi niṃdarī //

जानेहि नहीं मरमु सठ मोरा। मोर अहार जहाँ लगि चोरा।।jānehi nahīṃ maramu saṭha morā / mora ahāra jahā̃ lagi corā //

मुठिका एक महा कपि हनी। रुधिर बमत धरनीं ढनमनी।।muṭhikā eka mahā kapi hanī / rudhira bamata dharanīṃ ḍhanamanī //

पुनि संभारि उठि सो लंका। जोरि पानि कर बिनय संसका।।puni saṃbhāri uṭhi so laṃkā / jori pāni kara binaya saṃsakā //

जब रावनहि ब्रह्म बर दीन्हा। चलत बिरंचि कहा मोहि चीन्हा।।jaba rāvanahi brahma bara dīnhā / calata biraṃci kahā mohi cīnhā //

बिकल होसि तैं कपि कें मारे। तब जानेसु निसिचर संघारे।।bikala hosi taiṃ kapi keṃ māre / taba jānesu nisicara saṃghāre //

तात मोर अति पुन्य बहूता। देखेउँ नयन राम कर दूता।।tāta mora ati punya bahūtā / dekheũ nayana rāma kara dūtā //

Doha

तात स्वर्ग अपबर्ग सुख धरिअ तुला एक अंग।tāta svarga apabarga sukha dharia tulā eka aṃga /

तूल न ताहि सकल मिलि जो सुख लव सतसंग।।4।।tūla na tāhi sakala mili jo sukha lava satasaṃga //

Meaning Taking a mosquito-small form and remembering the Lord as man-lion, Hanuman set out for Lanka. A demoness, Lankini, said: you pass ignoring me? Fool, don't you know my secret? Every thief here is my food. The great monkey struck her once with his fist; she reeled to the ground, vomiting blood. Recovering, she joined her palms and pleaded in fear: when Brahma gave Ravana his boon, he left me a sign: when a monkey's blow distresses you, know the demons' end has come. Dear one, my merit is great: I have seen Rama's messenger with my own eyes. Heaven and liberation together on one pan of the scale cannot equal a moment's joy among the good.

Searching palace after palace, Hanuman finds a house marked with Rama's weapons and a tulsi plant, and hears its master wake chanting Rama's name. It is Vibhishana, Ravana's brother. Hanuman, disguised as a brahmin, speaks with him; they tell each other who they are, and Vibhishana says he lives among the demons like a tongue among teeth. Hanuman assures him of Rama's love for his devotees.

कपि करि हृदयँ बिचार दीन्हि मुद्रिका डारी तब।kapi kari hr̥dayã bicāra dīnhi mudrikā ḍārī taba /

Meaning Hands folded, Sita begged Trijata, her companion in trouble: 'Mother, help me die; separation is unbearable. Build a pyre, light it; prove my love true. Who can bear words like spears?' Trijata clasped her feet, told of the Lord's glory, said no fire is found at night, and left. Sita said: 'Fate is against me: no fire, no end of pain. Embers glow in the sky, yet no star falls; the fiery moon drips no fire, knowing me ill-fated. Ashoka, make your name true, end my sorrow; your new leaves are flame, give me fire.' Seeing her, Hanuman felt that moment an age. He dropped the ring; she rose and gladly took it, as if the Ashoka gave an ember.

Meaning She saw the lovely ring engraved with Rama's name and, startled, knew it, torn between joy and sorrow: who could defeat invincible Raghurai, yet no illusion could make such a ring. Meaning Knowing him Hari's devotee, Sita filled with love; her eyes brimmed, her body thrilled. 'Hanuman, I was drowning in a sea of separation; you became my ship. Tell me, are Khara's slayer and his brother well? Raghurai is gentle and kind; why so hard now? Does Raghunayak, who delights his servants, ever remember me? Will my eyes ever be soothed by his dark, gentle form?' Words failed, tears welled: 'Ah, lord, you have utterly forgotten me.' Seeing her so stricken, the monkey said humbly: 'Mother, the gracious Lord and his brother are well, but he grieves at your grief. Do not think his love less; Rama's love is twice yours. Take heart; hear Raghupati's message.' His voice choked, eyes filling.

निसिचर निकर पतंग सम रघुपति बान कृसानु।nisicara nikara pataṃga sama raghupati bāna kr̥sānu /

जननी हृदयँ धीर धरु जरे निसाचर जानु।।15।।jananī hr̥dayã dhīra dharu jare nisācara jānu //

Meaning Rama said: 'Sita, apart from you all things turn against me. New leaves seem fire, night the night of doom, the moon burns like the sun. Lotus groves are thickets of spears; clouds seem to rain hot oil. What soothed now hurts; the cool, fragrant breeze is a serpent's breath. Telling grief eases it, but whom can I tell? No one knows. Beloved, only my mind knows the truth of our love, and it stays always with you; know my love's essence from this.' Vaidehi, lost in love, forgot her body. Hanuman said: 'Take heart, mother; remember Rama, who delights his servants, recall Raghupati's power, and cast off fear. Demons are moths, Raghupati's arrows fire. Count them burned.'

Doha

सुनु माता साखामृग नहिं बल बुद्धि बिसाल।sunu mātā sākhāmr̥ga nahiṃ bala buddhi bisāla /

Meaning 'Had Raghubir known, Raghurai would not have delayed. Janaki, when the sun of Rama's arrows rises, where will the demon darkness be? I would take you now, mother, but by Rama, I lack the Lord's command. Be patient a few days; Raghubir will come with the monkeys, slay the demons and take you away, and Narada and others will sing his fame in three worlds.' Sita said: 'Son, are all monkeys like you? The demons are mighty; I doubt greatly.' Hanuman revealed his body, vast as a golden mountain, fearsome in battle, hugely strong. Sita took heart; the Wind's son shrank again. 'Mother, monkeys lack great strength or wit, but by the Lord's power a tiny snake can eat Garuda.'

Doha

देखि बुद्धि बल निपुन कपि कहेउ जानकीं जाहु।dekhi buddhi bala nipuna kapi kaheu jānakīṃ jāhu /

रघुपति चरन हृदयँ धरि तात मधुर फल खाहु।।17।।raghupati carana hr̥dayã dhari tāta madhura phala khāhu //

Meaning Hanuman's words, full of devotion, glory, radiance and strength, contented Sita. Knowing him dear to Rama, she blessed him: "Child, be a treasury of strength and virtue, ageless, deathless, rich in goodness; may Raghunayaka love you greatly." Hearing "may the Lord be gracious," Hanuman was lost in love. Bowing at her feet again and again, palms joined, he said: "Mother, now I am fulfilled; your blessing is famed as unfailing. Seeing the lovely fruit on these trees, I feel very hungry." "Son, mighty night-roaming warriors guard this grove." "Mother, I fear them not, if you are glad at heart." Seeing his wisdom and strength, Janaki said: "Go, child; keep Raghupati's feet in your heart and eat the sweet fruit."

Hanuman eats the fruit and tears up the trees; the guards who attack him are overthrown, and Ravana's son Akshaya is killed. Ravana sends Meghanada. When nothing else holds Hanuman, Meghanada looses the Brahma weapon, and Hanuman lets it bind him out of respect for it. Tied in serpent nooses, he is brought into Ravana's court, where the demons crowd in to watch.

Meaning The ocean said: the monkey brothers Nila and Nala were blessed by a sage in childhood; by your glory, mountains they touch will float. About this text

The verses are the Ramcharitmanas of Goswami Tulsidas (16th century), in the original Awadhi. The text was compared word by word across three digital editions. Two of them (WirelessAlien and DharmicData) both name the IIT Kanpur Ramcharitmanas project as their source, so GRETIL is the only separate check. Where they disagreed, the reading two editions share was kept, and nothing was corrected from memory. The closing verses of the Ayodhya, Aranya and Lanka Kands are found in GRETIL alone, where the other two editions stop early. The text has not yet been checked against a printed edition.

  • WirelessAlien, Ramcharitmanas in JSON (Unlicense): the main text.
  • Bhavy Khatri, DharmicData, from the IIT Kanpur Ramcharitmanas project (ODbL 1.0): second edition, and the opening shlokas.
  • GRETIL, Göttingen, input by a group of volunteers at Ratlam: independent third edition, used to confirm the text and the only source for those closing verses.

The roman transliteration is generated from the Devanagari in the scholarly convention GRETIL uses. Episode titles and summaries are this site's own short retellings and are not part of the text. Paintings are illustrations made for this site.

The meanings shown with the verses are this site's own plain renderings, written for this page; no published translation was used, and no named editorial review has been recorded yet. They are not part of the text; the verses remain the text.
