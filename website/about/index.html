About Spritual

Spritual is a free reading site of illustrated stories from the Ramayana, the Mahabharata and the Puranas, in English and Hindi. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and every story can be read from beginning to end without signing up.

Retellings, not scripture

Each story is a retelling in plain, modern English and Hindi. It follows an episode as the old texts tell it, but it is written for today’s reader. It is not a translation of the Sanskrit, and it does not replace the texts themselves or a teacher.

At the end of every story, under Sources & context, you will find the works it draws on, such as the Valmiki Ramayana with its book and chapter. Where a reference is still being checked, only the name of the work is shown until it is confirmed.

These retellings have not yet been reviewed by an outside scholar or teacher. Traditions tell these stories in many ways, and a retelling may follow one telling where your family or region follows another. If something reads wrong to you, please tell us.

The paintings

The paintings are generated illustrations, made with image-generation software in one consistent painted style and then colour-graded for this site. The gentle movement in the film clips is made from those same paintings. They are imagined scenes, not historical artworks or temple images, and a detail of dress, ornament or form may be wrong.

Suggest a correction

If a name, a detail, a word in Hindi or English, a source or a painting looks wrong, we want to know. Mention the story, the language and the scene number. Report a correction explains what helps most.

For now, corrections are sent through GitHub, which needs a free account, and reports there are public. We know this is not easy for every reader.

Your privacy

Spritual keeps your saved stories and reflections only in your own browser and sends nothing to us. Privacy & your reading lists everything it keeps and lets you erase it.
