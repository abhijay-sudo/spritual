## Brand & Style

This design system translates the quiet grandeur of Indian architectural heritage, illuminated manuscripts, and collectible hardbound folios into a modern digital platform. The visual world rejects generic spiritual clichés—incense swirls, synthetic purple neon, or amorphous gradients—in favor of structural discipline, archival linen textures, tectonic symmetry, and the exactitude of classical miniature painting.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Architectural Poise:** Inspired by Rajasthan's stepwells (baoris) and monolithic stone jharokhas; measured, rhythmic, grounded.
- **Archival Reverence:** The sensory feel of heavy rag cotton paper, gilded edges, hand-rubbed vermilion ink, and aged teal silk book bindings.
- **Editorial Decisiveness:** Stark contrasts, deliberate typographic scale jumps, and generous breathing room that convey cultural authority and contemporary sophistication.

### Design Movement
**Heritage Editorial with Tectonic Structuralism.** The interface treats screen space as an archival folio. Components are framed with razor-fine dual-stroke filigree, asymmetric margins, and stepped structural divisions echoing temple plan layouts. Surfaces sit flat and deliberate, using layered pigmented washes rather than simulated synthetic lighting.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural mineral pigments: crushed lapis and malachite yielding deep petrol teal, sun-cured saffron threads, pressed buttermilk wash, and burnt earth terracotta. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#123D3A` — Deep Petrol Teal):** The anchor of supreme authority and spiritual depth. Used for dominant headings, structural frames, key action triggers, and protective containers.
- **Secondary (`#D78A45` — Rich Kesari Saffron):** The flame of devotion and illumination. Reserved for active states, highlighted sacred dates, consecrated progress markers, and primary callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#EED9A1` — Pale Butter Sandstone):** The warm glow of brass lamps and temple sandstone. Used for soft ambient surface cards, gilded dividers, badge fills, and illuminated quotes.
- **Neutral (`#F6F3EB` — Warm Ivory Linen Ground):** The unbleached hand-spun base. Serves as the primary canvas ground across light surfaces, preventing clinical digital brightness.
- **Accent Complementary (`#8C3827` — Temple Terracotta):** Used selectively for sacred alerts, tab markers, and traditional marginalia annotations.

### Surface Color Logic
- **Base Canvas:** `#F6F3EB` (Ivory Linen).
- **Elevated Canvas Tier 1:** `#FFFFFF` with an opacity bleed over ivory for crisp card planes.
- **Sanctuary Mode (Inverse Dark):** For nocturnal readings and deep contemplative rituals, the system flips to Deep Petrol Teal ground (`#123D3A`), with `#EED9A1` text, `#D78A45` focal points, and translucent saffron overlays (`rgba(215, 138, 69, 0.12)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes monumental, classical discipline with razor-sharp modernist utility:

1. **The Display & Editorial Voice (`Noto Serif`):** Chosen for its sculpted serifs, classical proportions, and inherent cultural nobility without descending into kitsch. It handles long sacred verses, mantras, and declarative display banners.
2. **The Functional Body Voice (`Hanken Grotesk`):** A humanistic, precision-crafted grotesque that ensures long-form reading comfort, transaction clarity, and ergonomic legibility in smaller interface modules.
3. **The Metadata & Structural Voice (`Space Grotesk`):** Used in uppercase with tracked spacing (`letterSpacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) for timestamps, lunar tithes, shloka indices, chapter tags, and navigational breadcrumbs—imparting a calculated, archival catalog feel.

### Typographic Rules
- All top-level section titles must use `headline-lg` with an accompanying tracked `label-sm` numeral prefix (e.g., `04 / PRAYER CYCLE`).
- Body text containing scriptural excerpts switches dynamically to `body-lg` (`Noto Serif`) with a 1.875rem line-height to allow breathing room for contemplation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Disciplined Axial Grid** rooted in Indian mandala symmetry and editorial book margins:

### Grid Structure
- **Mobile (under 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Interactive triggers and primary rituals must sit within the bottom 40% thumb-reach zone.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12-column layout bounded by a max-width of `1280px` with generous `4rem` outer canvas margins, creating the expansive border of a framed miniature folio.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections deploy generous vertical strides (`space-2xl` or `4rem`) between distinct thematic modules to establish meditative pauses.
- Related controls and metadata clusters adhere strictly to compact intervals (`space-xs` to `space-md`).
- Stepwell geometric layouts use staggered column offsets (e.g., Column 1–7 for scripture, Column 9–12 for commentary and marginal notes).

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally avoids synthetic drop shadows and blurry light spreads. Depth is conveyed purely through **architectural stratification, tactile borders, and pigment density**:

### Depth Techniques
1. **Parchment Stratification:** Surfaces achieve elevation by shifting base tints. Base level is `#F6F3EB`; resting cards shift to pure `#FFFFFF` with fine pigmented bounds; active focus states reveal an underlying `#EED9A1` plane.
2. **Dual-Stroke Hairlines:** Elevated panels are framed with a hairline inner border (`1px solid rgba(18, 61, 58, 0.12)`) and an offset grounding border (`1px solid #123D3A`), evoking the crisp multi-rule framing of Mughal and Rajput miniature manuscripts.
3. **Stepwell Carved Recesses:** Depressed or inset states (e.g., input wells, active audio track lanes) use an inverted border tone (`rgba(18, 61, 58, 0.08)`) with a subtle tinted wash (`#EFECE3`), visually resting 1mm below the paper level.
4. **Mineral Shadow Tint:** Where floating elements (such as action sheets or devotional modal dialogs) require detachment, the shadow is rendered with dense terracotta/teal tinting rather than neutral grey: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 61, 58, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry, reflecting cut stone masonry, hand-trimmed parchment, and bevelled wood blocks rather than modern synthetic "bubble" surfaces.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Standard Corners (`0.25rem`):** Buttons, editorial cards, input fields, and banners. This subtle rounding preserves the crisp corner integrity of architectural plinths while eliminating harsh digital pixels.
- **Stepwell & Arch Insets:** Bespoke container headers use geometric notched chamfers (45-degree corner steps of 8px) or pointed arch crests (cusped torana arches) drawn as single-path SVG masks.
- **Full Radius (Pill):** Strictly limited to numeric status badges, step indicators, and tactile audio scrub handles.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Consecrated Action:** Solid Petrol Teal (`#123D3A`) fill with Pale Butter (`#EED9A1`) typography in `Space Grotesk` uppercase. Sharp 0.25rem radius. Rimmed with a hairline 1px border of `#D78A45` (Saffron). On press, background shifts to Terracotta (`#8C3827`).
- **Secondary / Archival Outline:** Transparent fill, 1.5px solid `#123D3A` border, Teal text. Hover introduces a delicate `#EED9A1` pale wash.
- **Floating Thumb Triggers:** Positioned anchored at bottom-center on mobile screens, framed in a dual-line golden halo, allowing one-hand thumb devotion without reaching.

### Cards (Manuscript Folios)
- **Structure:** Never generic rounded white cards with soft shadows. Folios feature an ivory ground, framed with a 1px teal hairline border. The top right corner includes an inscribed numeric index in `label-sm` (e.g., `§ 108`).
- **Accent Cards:** Sacred quotes and daily darshan cards use the Pale Butter tint (`#EED9A1`) with an inset saffron corner bracket motif reminiscent of illuminated book corners.

### Chips & Sacred Filter Tokens
- Flat `#F6F3EB` ground with 1px solid `rgba(18, 61, 58, 0.2)` borders.
- Selected state fills with Deep Petrol Teal (`#123D3A`), text switches to Pale Butter (`#EED9A1`), punctuated with an ochre saffron dot indicator.

### Input Fields
- Deep parchment wells with flat bottom border (1.5px `#123D3A`) instead of fully rounded input boxes.
- Floating labels in `label-sm` (`Space Grotesk`), transitioning into Terracotta (`#8C3827`) when focused.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Radio Buttons:** Architectural concentric circles. Unchecked is a 1.5px teal ring; checked reveals a solid saffron jewel core (`#D78A45`).
- **Checkboxes:** Square diamond orientation (rotated 45 degrees), echoing sacred yantra geometry. Checked state displays a crisp geometric dot in place of a generic checkmark.

### Bespoke Cultural Components
- **Chant & Audio Rosary Player:** Tactile stepped track representing 108 beads, where current position fills with burnished gold.
- **Jharokha Frame:** An editorial image container featuring an SVG-masked pointed trefoil arch header for iconography and sacred imagery.
- **Stepwell Progress Rail:** Replaces standard horizontal progress bars with alternating downward zig-zag geometric steps.
