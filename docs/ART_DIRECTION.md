# Spritual editorial art — local preview specification

**Direction:** sacred cinematic editorial. Use painterly Indian devotional imagery with tactile texture, natural light, restrained charcoal/mineral/ivory/brass colour and rare saffron. A still frame should feel contemplative, not like glossy fantasy, stock photography, a game character, or a copied competitor image. Avoid text baked into art, celebrity likenesses, extra limbs, arbitrary symbols, inconsistent attributes and overdecorated backgrounds.

**Composition:** keep faces and iconographic attributes clear at 320 px. Save a focal point per asset and verify 4:5 portrait, square discovery crop and wide hero before approval. Reserve darker visual space for short labels; long explanations belong below the image. One strong image can lead Today, a Divine entry or a story; scripture reading and privacy/settings remain text-first. Stable media should be reused across Explore, search and Saved.

**Review gate:** all current art is an **unreviewed local preview**. A named human reviewer must check identity, iconography, tradition-specific claims, anatomy, crop, alt text, provenance and intended usage rights before publication. Generated art is not evidence of religious or historical accuracy. `mediaManifest.json` records asset paths, dimensions, focal points, bilingual alt text and current review/rights status; `npm run media:audit` checks coverage and file integrity. `npm run media:release-preflight` must continue to block until rights and review are established. Do not change those fields merely to pass a test.
