# Competitor experience check — 25 September 2026

## 26 September calm-experience implication

Official product descriptions remain evidence of *advertised capability*, not observed retention or installed-app quality. [Bhagavad Gita For All](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/bhagavad-gita-for-all-video/id6504813763) already advertises daily verse/video, topic access, layered explanation and AI; a topic search alone is not a distinctive member promise. [Calm's official help](https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005140414-What-are-the-Calm-Dailies-Daily-Meditations-Movement) shows the value of a recognizable daily entry, while [YouVersion](https://www.youversion.com/bible-app) makes bookmarks/notes a familiar part of scripture use. [Apple Books reading controls](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/read-books-iphc1af7c57/27/ios/27) illustrate that type size and comfortable reading are baseline expectations. The current alpha therefore tests one reading with direct source, interpretation status, optional action and private continuation, instead of claiming another daily-content feed or AI catalogue. This is a product hypothesis, not validated customer preference.

Scope: current App Store product descriptions and visible promotional screenshots, plus the repository's earlier competitor dossier. These are vendor claims and design observations, **not** installed-app usability sessions, motion recordings, conversion evidence or customer interviews.

| App | What the current product shows/offers | Design lesson for Spritual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bhagavad Gita For All](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/bhagavad-gita-for-all-video/id6504813763) | Daily shloka, video explanation, chapter/topic navigation, six interpretation layers and source-referencing AI chat. Marketing screenshots place illustrations and a clear “Start your day” action first. | A plain verse dictionary or generic AI chat is not differentiated. Make the original text, context and one action easy to traverse; do not claim production-grade video or AI from a static prototype. |
| [Vedapath](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/vedapath-hindu-scriptures/id6742242873) | Advertises 30+ texts, many languages, comics, microdramas, maps and AI. | Catalogue breadth is already contested. Publish fewer well-sourced, reviewed texts and expose exact available count instead of implying an all-Vedas library exists. |
| [Sri Mandir](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/sri-mandir-puja-chadhava/id1637621461) | Puja/chadhava booking, temples, devotional media and a virtual temple. | Ritual commerce is a different operations and trust business. Do not graft unfulfilled puja/service buttons onto a learning product. |
| [Hallow](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/hallow-prayer-meditation/id1405323394) | Daily prayer, religious calendar challenges, guided audio, multiple devotional formats and familiar teacher voices. | The repeat habit is attached to real substance and trusted voices. A synthetic sound test is engineering proof, not an attractive member proposition. |
| [Calm](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/calm-sleep-meditation/id571800810) | Daily Calm, soundscapes, sleep stories and structured programs. | A calm visual language works when the content is genuinely useful; decoration cannot replace it. |
| [Insight Timer](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/insight-timer-meditate-sleep/id337472899) | Teacher follows, short sessions, music, groups and milestones. | Teacher relationships and choice are established patterns; Spritual must earn trust through source and human review rather than call these unique inventions. |

The rejected `/alpha` front door led with a 20-second synthetic tone, invented local identity, “audio fixture,” and no sacred text or interpretive value. The running screen matched that diagnosis. It was a test surface for the delivery workflow, not an experience to compete for a person's daily attention.

**Implemented design response:** `/alpha` and `/alpha/today` now present a scripture-led entry. Source-linked Gita text, transliteration, original explicitly unreviewed meaning, a small real-life action, English/Hindi switching, situation-based access to the three actual lessons, local carryover and a gentle return check-in form the first complete journey. A restrained illustrated cover and motion for stage changes add warmth; reduced-motion preferences remove effects. The teacher/community workflow remains in Circle. No real audio or additional scripture is fabricated. The best future advantage remains a hypothesis to test with readers and teachers, not a claim established by this scan.

Motion limitation: App Store screenshots establish composition and content hierarchy but do not reveal actual animation timing or behavior. This implementation's motion was judged in the running local app, not copied from an unobserved competitor animation.

## Life → Wisdom market check — 25 September 2026

This pass reads current official product and store descriptions, not installed journeys. Feature lists below are developer claims; they do not establish use, satisfaction, retention or revenue.

| Product | Specific mechanic visible in its official listing | Consequence for this alpha |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bhagavad Gita For All](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/bhagavad-gita-for-all-video/id6504813763) | Browse by life topic *or* chapter/verse; each verse has Sanskrit, context, explanation and personal/family/professional application. It advertises situation-based AI with verse references and a daily video shloka. | **Life → verse → application already exists.** A topic chooser, daily verse or cited chat alone is not a differentiator. Make the source and editorial status legible, and show why a passage matched before asking a reader to act. |
| [Sri Mandir](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/sri-mandir-puja-chadhava/id1637621461) | Virtual temple plus devotional media; named/gotra temple puja and chadhava bookings produce a video or certificate and sometimes prasad delivery. | Situation-based help here often ends in a fulfilled ritual. Keep learning/reflection distinct from a service order or a promise of an outcome. |
| [VAMA](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/vama-puja-chadhava-astro-tv/id1613296409) | Combines temple booking, human astrologer chat, VAMA TV mythology/fiction/audiobooks, calendar and retail. Its listing explicitly maps life problems to puja products. | A broad spiritual super-app and video catalogue are occupied territory. Label storytelling as retelling and cite its source lineage; avoid implying a ritual solves debt, health or relationships. |
| [Miracle of Mind by Isha](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/miracle-of-mind-sadhguru/id6737795677) | One free guided seven-minute meditation by a named teacher, adjustable duration, reminder, wisdom snippets and milestones/streaks. | Daily return can center on one trusted practice. Spritual's return cue should be a useful reading/action, with no missed-day penalty or fabricated teacher voice. |
| [Saged](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/saged-daily-spiritual-rituals/id1436713096) | Daily personalized astrology/moon ritual, oracle/gratitude practices, multi-day journeys and live classes. | “Daily ritual” and personalization are established. Prefer user-chosen life situations and bounded, source-linked paths over inferred belief or cosmic profiling. |
| [Utsav](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.applex.utsav) | Life-problem entry points connect to temple pujas; booking returns ritual video and delivered prasad. Its [iOS listing](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/utsav-puja-mandir-gyan/id6739375483) also foregrounds puja videos. | This is a transactional fulfillment loop. Spritual should describe an optional everyday practice accurately and avoid promising material results. |
| [BBT Transcend](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/transcend/id6448913630) | Large owned-book library across languages with read/listen, direct verse/chapter navigation, search, flashcards, offline lectures and book-grounded AI. | “AI over scripture,” Hindi support and read/listen are already offered. Rights-cleared editions and precise citations matter more than claiming corpus breadth. |
| [YouVersion Bible](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/bible/id282935706) | Verse of the Day, topic-based plans, guided reflection, private or shared notes, bookmarks, reading controls and plans with friends. | A plan can connect reading to return; private reflection must remain the default, with deliberate sharing and an exact resume path. |
| [Bhagavad Gītā by bgita.app](https://bgita.app/get) | Its official site advertises plain-language meaning search that reveals *why* a verse matched through translation, commentary or word-level meaning. | Even explainable semantic retrieval is not wholly novel. The defensible test is whether readers trust the passage selection and can actually carry one source-grounded action into life. |

**Product implication:** The first credible Life → Wisdom journey should accept a user-chosen situation, return only passages the app actually holds, explain the match, distinguish original Sanskrit from unreviewed editorial interpretation, and offer one optional small action. Exact source links and a graceful “no supported match” state are more important than a broad but unlicensed catalogue. This is a design hypothesis to test with readers, not a market-winning claim.
