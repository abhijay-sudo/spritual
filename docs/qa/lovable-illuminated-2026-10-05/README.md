The leaf-turn control is a real button-driven interaction rather than a static
ornament: it updates the folio anchor, animates a perspective leaf, moves focus to
the next folio and announces completion. With reduced motion it performs the same
navigation and focus transfer without creating the animated leaf.

The implementation now vendors the exact observed Latin families—Cormorant Garamond
and Manrope—as optimized local WOFF2 files with their OFL licenses and checksums in
`website/assets/REFERENCE_FONTS.md`. Existing Anek/Tiro assets continue to render
Devanagari. Browser checks confirm both reference families load and that the reader
keeps a usable left and right leaf at 768px. At two-leaf tablet widths the passage
map uses one readable column instead of squeezing two columns into the narrow leaf:
automated checks cover 768/820/900/1023px, require more than 120px of label width and
permit no more than two lines. The final independent inspection measured 215px-wide
rows with one-line labels at 768px. Reflection copy and folio controls are checked at
WCAG AA normal-text contrast across Light, Sepia and Dark.
